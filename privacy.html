Arise — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Arise ("the app") is an alarm clock for Muslims. This policy explains what information the app uses and how.

Summary: Arise has no account, no ads and no analytics. Your alarms, location and prayer data stay on your phone. We never sell or share them.

What the app uses, and where it stays

Apart from subscription status (handled by Google Play and RevenueCat, see revenuecat.com/privacy), the app does not send any of this information to us or anyone else. It has no advertising, analytics or tracking code. It does not access your contacts, photos, microphone or files.

Permissions

Alarms & reminders, notifications, full-screen alarm and approximate location (only if you choose it). Each is used only for the purpose shown when the app asks.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its data in your phone's settings. Because nothing is stored on our servers, there is nothing else to delete.

Children

Arise is meant for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Changes

If a future version adds features that send data (for example optional backup or Fajr Squad), this policy will be updated first and the app will ask for your consent.

Contact

Use "Contact support" in the app's settings, or email the developer address shown on Arise's Google Play page. We reply within 7 days.
