What Arise does

Arise is an alarm clock with wake-up missions, prayer times and a Fajr streak. Prayer times are calculated on your phone from your location and the method you choose. They are estimates. Check them against your local mosque if exact times matter to you.

Subscriptions

  • Arise needs an active subscription to create and edit alarms. Plans are billed through Google Play.
  • Both plans may start with a 3-day free trial, one trial per person. If you do not cancel before the trial ends, Google Play charges the plan's price.
  • The monthly plan renews every month and the yearly plan every year, until you cancel.
  • You can cancel at any time in Google Play, under Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.
  • Prices may change. Google Play will tell you before a new price applies to you.

Using the alarm safely

  • No alarm app can promise to ring in every situation. A phone that is switched off, out of battery, muted by the system or restricted by battery-saving settings may not ring. Use the reliability check in the app and keep a backup alarm for anything critical.
  • Missions ask you to move, walk or shake your phone. Do them only where it is safe.
  • The emergency stop is always available. Use it whenever you need to.
